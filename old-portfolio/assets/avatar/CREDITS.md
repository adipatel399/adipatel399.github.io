# Portrait source and licenses

The portrait was assembled, posed, rigged, and rendered in Blender 5.2.1.

## MakeHuman assets — CC0 1.0

- Human mesh (`hm08`), young male morph targets, skeleton weights and eye mesh:
  https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman/tree/master/makehuman/data
- Young light-skinned male diffuse texture, eyebrow002, short01 hairstyle (with a subtle short02 strand normal), and male_casualsuit06
  (T-shirt component only):
  https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/makehuman_system_assets.html
- System pack source:
  https://files.makehumancommunity.org/asset_packs/makehuman_system_assets/makehuman_system_assets_cc0.zip
- Asset license:
  https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman/blob/master/LICENSE.ASSETS.md
  A local copy is retained at `tools/avatar-source/LICENSE.ASSETS.md`.

The assets credit Data Collection AB, Joel Palmius, Jonas Hauquier and the
MakeHuman contributors. They were released under CC0 in September 2020.

Changes include young male proportions, arm/wrist/finger pose, removal of the
lower body below the abdomen, a subtle closed-mouth smile, light-blue polo material with a modeled folded collar and button placket,
optimized textures, a weighted head/neck
rig, and Draco compression. The backward six-panel baseball cap with an
adjustment strap and rear visor, sunglasses, studio lighting, and web interaction
were authored for this portfolio. This is an illustrated character based on the
requested appearance; it is not a scan of the portfolio owner.
